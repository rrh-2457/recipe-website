Sunrise Avocado Toast

A wholesome breakfast made with toasted whole-grain bread, creamy avocado, fresh herbs, and a touch of lemon.

Preparation Time: 10 Minutes

Cooking Time: 5 Minutes

Servings: 2

Ingredients

  • 2 slices whole-grain bread
  • 1 ripe avocado
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice
  • Salt to taste
  • Black pepper
  • Chili flakes
  • Fresh coriander

Instructions

  1. Toast the bread until crisp.
  2. Mash the avocado in a bowl.
  3. Add lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  4. Spread the avocado mixture evenly over the toast.
  5. Sprinkle chili flakes and chopped coriander.
  6. Serve immediately while fresh.

Nutrition (Approximate)

  • Calories: 240 kcal
  • Protein: 7 g
  • Carbohydrates: 24 g
  • Fat: 13 g
  • Fiber: 8 g
