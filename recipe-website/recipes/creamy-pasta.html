Creamy Tomato Pasta

A comforting pasta dish prepared with a rich tomato sauce, fresh herbs, garlic, and cream. Ideal for a quick homemade lunch.

Preparation Time: 10 Minutes

Cooking Time: 20 Minutes

Servings: 2

Ingredients

  • 200 g pasta
  • 2 tomatoes, pureed
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons fresh cream
  • 1 teaspoon mixed herbs
  • 2 tablespoons grated cheese
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Fresh basil for garnish

Instructions

  1. Boil the pasta until tender and drain.
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan.
  3. Sauté garlic for one minute.
  4. Add tomato puree and cook for 5–7 minutes.
  5. Mix in cream, herbs, salt, and pepper.
  6. Add the cooked pasta and toss well.
  7. Sprinkle grated cheese and garnish with basil.
  8. Serve hot.

Nutrition (Approx.)

  • Calories: 430 kcal
  • Protein: 14 g
  • Carbohydrates: 55 g
  • Fat: 17 g
  • Fiber: 5 g
