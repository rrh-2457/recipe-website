Herb Rice Bowl

A colorful rice bowl loaded with fresh vegetables, herbs, and a light lemon dressing. Perfect for a healthy and satisfying lunch.

Preparation Time: 15 Minutes

Cooking Time: 20 Minutes

Servings: 2

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 1/2 cup sweet corn
  • 1/2 cup peas
  • 1/2 capsicum, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon mixed herbs
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in a pan.
  2. Add carrot, peas, corn and capsicum.
  3. Cook for 5 minutes until slightly tender.
  4. Add cooked rice and mix well.
  5. Season with herbs, salt and pepper.
  6. Add lemon juice and stir.
  7. Serve warm.

Nutrition (Approx.)

  • Calories: 360 kcal
  • Protein: 9 g
  • Carbohydrates: 58 g
  • Fat: 10 g
  • Fiber: 6 g
