Coconut Vegetable Curry

A rich and creamy vegetable curry simmered in coconut milk with aromatic spices. This comforting dish pairs perfectly with steamed rice or flatbread.

Preparation Time: 15 Minutes

Cooking Time: 25 Minutes

Servings: 2

Ingredients

  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • 1 potato, diced
  • 1 carrot, sliced
  • 1/2 cup green peas
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon ginger paste
  • 1 tablespoon curry powder
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander for garnish

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a pan.
  2. Sauté onion, garlic, and ginger until fragrant.
  3. Add curry powder and cook for one minute.
  4. Add potato, carrot, and peas.
  5. Pour in coconut milk and ½ cup water.
  6. Cover and simmer for 20 minutes.
  7. Season with salt.
  8. Garnish with coriander and serve with rice.

Nutrition (Approx.)

  • Calories: 380 kcal
  • Protein: 8 g
  • Carbohydrates: 32 g
  • Fat: 24 g
  • Fiber: 7 g
