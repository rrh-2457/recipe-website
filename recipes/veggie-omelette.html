Garden Veggie Omelette

A fluffy omelette packed with fresh vegetables, herbs, and simple seasonings for a healthy breakfast.

Preparation Time: 10 Minutes

Cooking Time: 10 Minutes

Servings: 2

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1/4 cup spinach, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons capsicum, chopped
  • 1 teaspoon butter
  • Salt to taste
  • Black pepper
  • Mixed herbs

Instructions

  1. Beat the eggs in a bowl.
  2. Add vegetables, salt, pepper, and herbs.
  3. Heat butter in a non-stick pan.
  4. Pour the egg mixture evenly.
  5. Cook until the bottom is golden.
  6. Fold the omelette and cook for another minute.
  7. Serve hot with toasted bread.

Nutrition (Approximate)

  • Calories: 285 kcal
  • Protein: 18 g
  • Carbohydrates: 7 g
  • Fat: 20 g
  • Fiber: 2 g
