Stuffed Bell Peppers

Colorful bell peppers filled with seasoned rice, vegetables, and cheese, then baked until tender. A complete and satisfying dinner.

Preparation Time: 20 Minutes

Cooking Time: 30 Minutes

Servings: 2

Ingredients

  • 2 large bell peppers
  • 1½ cups cooked rice
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1/2 cup sweet corn
  • 1/2 cup grated cheese
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Cut the tops off the bell peppers and remove the seeds.
  3. Heat oil and sauté onion and tomato.
  4. Add corn, cooked rice, seasoning, salt, and pepper.
  5. Fill each pepper with the rice mixture.
  6. Top with grated cheese.
  7. Bake for 25–30 minutes.
  8. Serve hot.

Nutrition (Approx.)

  • Calories: 410 kcal
  • Protein: 13 g
  • Carbohydrates: 46 g
  • Fat: 18 g
  • Fiber: 7 g
