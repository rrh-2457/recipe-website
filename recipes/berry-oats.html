Berry Oat Bowl

A nutritious breakfast bowl made with rolled oats, fresh berries, banana, and honey.

Preparation Time: 5 Minutes

Cooking Time: 10 Minutes

Servings: 2

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 2 cups milk
  • 1 banana, sliced
  • ½ cup mixed berries
  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • 1 teaspoon chia seeds

Instructions

  1. Bring the milk to a gentle boil.
  2. Add the oats and cook for 5–7 minutes.
  3. Transfer to serving bowls.
  4. Top with banana slices and berries.
  5. Drizzle with honey.
  6. Sprinkle chia seeds before serving.

Nutrition (Approximate)

  • Calories: 310 kcal
  • Protein: 11 g
  • Carbohydrates: 49 g
  • Fat: 8 g
  • Fiber: 9 g
