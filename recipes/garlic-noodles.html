Garlic Vegetable Noodles

A quick and flavorful noodle dish made with fresh vegetables, garlic, and light seasonings. Perfect for a simple homemade dinner.

Preparation Time: 15 Minutes

Cooking Time: 15 Minutes

Servings: 2

Ingredients

  • 200 g noodles
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 carrot, julienned
  • 1 capsicum, sliced
  • 1/2 cup cabbage, shredded
  • 2 spring onions, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Cook the noodles according to package instructions and drain.
  2. Heat olive oil in a wok or pan.
  3. Sauté the garlic until fragrant.
  4. Add carrot, cabbage, and capsicum. Stir-fry for 4–5 minutes.
  5. Add the cooked noodles.
  6. Mix in soy sauce, salt, and pepper.
  7. Toss well and cook for 2 more minutes.
  8. Garnish with spring onions and serve hot.

Nutrition (Approx.)

  • Calories: 390 kcal
  • Protein: 11 g
  • Carbohydrates: 60 g
  • Fat: 12 g
  • Fiber: 5 g
