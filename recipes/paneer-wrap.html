Grilled Paneer Wrap

A soft tortilla filled with grilled paneer, crisp vegetables, and a refreshing mint yogurt dressing. A delicious and protein-rich lunch option.

Preparation Time: 15 Minutes

Cooking Time: 10 Minutes

Servings: 2

Ingredients

  • 2 whole wheat tortillas
  • 200 g paneer, cubed
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 capsicum, sliced
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 2 lettuce leaves
  • 2 tablespoons yogurt
  • 1 teaspoon mint chutney
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in a pan.
  2. Grill the paneer until lightly golden.
  3. Add onion and capsicum and cook for 3–4 minutes.
  4. Mix yogurt with mint chutney.
  5. Spread the dressing over each tortilla.
  6. Add lettuce, carrot, and the grilled paneer mixture.
  7. Roll the tortilla tightly into a wrap.
  8. Slice in half and serve warm.

Nutrition (Approx.)

  • Calories: 420 kcal
  • Protein: 21 g
  • Carbohydrates: 31 g
  • Fat: 23 g
  • Fiber: 5 g
